Strategy Features

Everything Built
Into the System

Every feature listed below is already coded inside the strategy — no extra tools or add-ons required.

🔲

BR 1 & BR 2 Zone Detection

Detects high-probability rejection zones based on swing structure. BR 1 is the primary zone, BR 2 is the equilibrium fallback. Each zone tracks FRESH / USED / INVALID status in real-time.

📐

Swing Structure Detection

Automatically detects bullish and bearish Break of Structure (BOS/CHoCH) using a configurable swing length. Draws break lines and labels directly on the chart.

📡

Multi-Timeframe Trend Filter

Filters entries using a higher-timeframe MA (EMA, SMA, WMA, or HMA up to 200 period). Blocks counter-trend signals automatically. Configurable timeframe — Daily, Weekly, 4H, 1H.

🔒

Auto Break-Even After TP1

Once TP1 is hit, the SL automatically moves to entry price (with optional buffer %). The remaining position becomes risk-free and rides toward TP2 and TP3.

🧭

Trade Direction Control

Set the EA/strategy to trade Long Only, Short Only, or Both directions. Useful for adapting to specific market conditions or broker restrictions.

✅

Live Hit Detection & Alerts

Visual markers appear on-chart when TP1, TP2, TP3, or SL is hit. Background flashes in color. Full alert system for TradingView: TP hits, SL hits, BOS/CHoCH signals, zone entries.

📋

Live Zone Dashboard

On-chart table shows real-time zone status for BR 1 and BR 2 — Trend filter, Swing Bias, Zone validity, Freshness status — for both Long and Short, updated every bar.

📏

SL/TP Line Visualizer

On every new entry, the system draws labeled SL, TP1, TP2, TP3, and Entry lines extending forward on the chart with configurable bar length.
